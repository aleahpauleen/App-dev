# My Favorite K-Drama: Queen of Tears

## About the Drama
**Queen of Tears** is a Korean romantic drama about Baek Hyun-woo and Hong Hae-in, a married couple who experience challenges in their relationship. Despite their misunderstandings and personal struggles, they slowly rediscover their love for each other.

## Main Characters
- Hong Hae-in
- Baek Hyun-woo
- Yoon Eun-sung
- Hong Soo-cheol
- Cheon Da-hye

## Why I Like This K-Drama
I like this drama because it has a beautiful love story, emotional scenes, and unexpected plot twists. It shows that relationships are not always perfect and that love requires understanding, patience, and sacrifice.

## My Favorite Character
**Hong Hae-in** is my favorite character because she is strong, independent, and determined. Even when she faces difficult situations, she tries her best to stay strong.
